# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Manager

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers coordinate cross-team execution and dependency management to keep delivery plans realistic and on track.

### Core Responsibilities
- Align delivery timelines across teams and workstreams
- Track cross-team dependencies, blockers, and handoffs
- Escalate delivery risks and support mitigation planning
- Ensure delivery commitments are visible and measurable

### Goals
- Maintain predictable delivery across teams
- Reduce delays caused by unresolved dependencies
- Improve coordination and accountability during execution

### Typical Communication
- Weekly cross-team delivery syncs
- Dependency and blocker updates in project channels
- Escalation summaries for project and product leadership

### Interaction with Existing Personas
- **Developers:** Coordinates sequencing and readiness across teams so development work can progress without avoidable blockers.
- **Product Managers:** Aligns scope and priority changes with current delivery capacity and dependency realities.
- **Project Managers:** Partners on schedule health, risk tracking, and escalation paths for cross-team execution issues.

### Lifecycle Support (Planning, Execution, Release, Continuous Improvement)
- **Planning:** Validates delivery feasibility, dependency order, and milestone alignment.
- **Execution:** Monitors progress across teams and resolves dependency conflicts quickly.
- **Release:** Confirms cross-team readiness and outstanding risks before release windows.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Reviews delivery outcomes and recurring coordination gaps to improve future planning.

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own technical direction and implementation alignment, making key engineering risk and trade-off decisions.

### Core Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and architecture guardrails
- Align implementation patterns across developers and teams
- Identify engineering risks and lead mitigation decisions
- Guide technical quality standards, reviews, and readiness

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, scalable solutions aligned to requirements
- Reduce engineering rework through early technical alignment
- Make timely, transparent decisions on technical trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and implementation briefings
- Risk and trade-off discussions with project and product partners
- Ongoing engineering guidance in planning and execution meetings

### Interaction with Existing Personas
- **Developers:** Provides technical direction, review support, and risk-based decision guidance during implementation.
- **Product Managers:** Translates product goals into feasible technical options and clarifies trade-offs.
- **Project Managers:** Aligns technical milestones, risk impacts, and decision timelines with project plans.

### Lifecycle Support (Planning, Execution, Release, Continuous Improvement)
- **Planning:** Shapes solution design, estimates technical complexity, and flags high-risk areas.
- **Execution:** Ensures implementation consistency and adjusts technical direction as new constraints emerge.
- **Release:** Verifies technical readiness, stability concerns, and deployment risk controls.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Leads technical retrospectives and drives architecture and engineering practice improvements.

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define testing strategy, quality gates, and validation responsibilities to ensure releases meet agreed quality standards.

### Core Responsibilities
- Define test strategy across functional, integration, and regression coverage
- Establish quality gates and entry/exit criteria for release readiness
- Coordinate validation responsibilities across QA and engineering contributors
- Track defects, quality risks, and remediation progress

### Goals
- Prevent critical defects from reaching production
- Improve confidence in release quality and system behavior
- Create clear and repeatable validation standards

### Typical Communication
- Test plan and quality gate reviews
- Defect triage summaries and quality risk updates
- Release readiness sign-off and validation status reports

### Interaction with Existing Personas
- **Developers:** Aligns on testability, defect resolution priorities, and quality expectations throughout delivery.
- **Product Managers:** Confirms validation coverage against acceptance criteria and expected user outcomes.
- **Project Managers:** Coordinates test timelines, quality risks, and release go/no-go dependencies.

### Lifecycle Support (Planning, Execution, Release, Continuous Improvement)
- **Planning:** Defines test scope, quality criteria, and validation dependencies early.
- **Execution:** Oversees test execution progress, defect trends, and risk-driven coverage adjustments.
- **Release:** Validates quality gate completion and provides release readiness recommendations.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Uses defect and escape analysis to improve testing strategy and team practices.

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons manage communication between delivery teams and stakeholder groups, ensuring priorities, feedback, and decisions remain aligned.

### Core Responsibilities
- Communicate priorities, status, and decisions to stakeholder groups
- Gather feedback and surface changes in expectations early
- Support decision-making by clarifying context, impacts, and trade-offs
- Maintain a clear communication cadence across project phases

### Goals
- Strengthen stakeholder trust and alignment
- Reduce ambiguity in priorities and decision ownership
- Improve feedback quality and responsiveness across teams

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and decision-readout sessions
- Feedback collection loops and follow-up summaries
- Priority and scope alignment updates with delivery leadership

### Interaction with Existing Personas
- **Developers:** Provides clear context on stakeholder priorities and shares implementation-impact feedback.
- **Product Managers:** Aligns external feedback with product direction and supports priority clarification.
- **Project Managers:** Coordinates communication cadence, escalation messaging, and decision tracking.

### Lifecycle Support (Planning, Execution, Release, Continuous Improvement)
- **Planning:** Confirms stakeholder expectations, constraints, and success criteria before execution starts.
- **Execution:** Maintains regular updates and gathers timely feedback on progress and trade-offs.
- **Release:** Communicates release readiness, known limitations, and post-release expectations.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Captures stakeholder insights and feeds them into retrospectives and roadmap refinements.
